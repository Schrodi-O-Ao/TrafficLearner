04 / Read the experiment

How to interpret the comparison

A picture helps explain a run. A controlled input and clearly defined measures make its result meaningful.

01

One demand schedule

Arrival times are sampled once from the scenario's rates and both controllers receive those same timestamps, which makes the comparison paired. The learned policy was trained earlier on different seeds; this page replays a saved policy and never trains.

02

Two decision rules

Fixed-time control ends every green after 18 seconds. The learned policy picks keep or switch from queue sizes, signal phase, and phase age, and a rule layer enforces a 10 second minimum green and a 60 second maximum green.

03

Measures need honest definitions

Experienced wait counts vehicles still queued at the end, so a controller cannot look better by leaving traffic behind. Completed-vehicle wait ignores them and is shown only in the README.

This is a one-intersection prototype. A single replay is one traffic sample; the README reports 30 paired seeds per scenario, including the scenarios where the learned policy loses.
