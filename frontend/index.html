04 / Methodology

Evaluation Principles

Strict experimental controls ensure every measured difference is attributable to policy choices rather than random variation.

01

Paired Seeded Schedule

Arrival timestamps are generated once per scenario run. Both the fixed-time controller and the Q-learning policy face the exact same vehicle arrivals, eliminating sampling bias.

02

Bounded Decision Models

The baseline operates a strict 18-second fixed green cycle. The reinforcement learning agent decides when to switch or hold based on observed queue lengths, bounded by safety rules (10s min, 60s max green).

03

Honest Delay Accounting

Experienced wait metrics account for vehicles still stranded in queues at the end of the simulation. A controller cannot artificially inflate its performance by abandoning traffic.
